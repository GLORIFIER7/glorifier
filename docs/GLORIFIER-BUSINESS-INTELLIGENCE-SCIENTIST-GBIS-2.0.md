# GLORIFIER Business Intelligence Scientist — GBIS-2.0

## Refined architecture

GLORIFIER Business Intelligence is upgraded from dashboard reporting into a continuous intelligence operating system.

The design synthesizes modern data strategy, semantic BI, data-product operating models, AI operating models, data monetization and governed decision intelligence. AWS guidance emphasizes integrated scalable storage, mandatory security, catalog/glossary governance, fit-for-purpose services, AI/ML, business abstractions and measurable outcomes. Microsoft describes the BI semantic layer as the layer that standardizes business concepts, relationships, calculations and rules. McKinsey's research emphasizes treating data as products, continuously improving those products, and connecting AI operating models to measurable business outcomes. 

## Domains

Market, competitive, customer, product, sales, marketing, operations, financial, pricing, supply chain, risk, workforce, technology, cloud, cybersecurity, data, regulatory, IP, assets, IoT, ecosystem, partners, marketplace, revenue, scenarios, early-warning and opportunity intelligence.

## Continuous cycle

Discover → Ingest → Normalize → Validate → Catalog → Model → Observe → Explain → Predict → Simulate → Prioritize → Recommend → Govern → Measure → Learn → Improve

## 24/7 architecture

Continuous operation means the system can continuously observe authorized sources, update evidence, detect changes, recompute metrics, generate signals and prepare recommendations.

It does NOT mean unrestricted autonomous financial, legal, contractual, destructive or irreversible actions.

Watchlists support scheduled/event-driven cadence. Consequential actions continue through the Revenue Control Plane and Human Authority.

- Customer and product intelligence

## Economic truth

All estimates, forecasts, opportunity values, market values, savings estimates and expected values remain explicitly separate from verified revenue.

Verified revenue continues to require qualifying payment evidence and external references through the Revenue Control Plane.
