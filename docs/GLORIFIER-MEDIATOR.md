# GLORIFIER Mediator

## Purpose
GLORIFIER Mediator is the explicit top-level provider-neutral mediation layer connecting AI architectures, specialist capabilities, external demand, marketplaces, execution connectors, evidence systems and settlement rails.

## Operating model
Human Owner → AI CEO → Mediator → Capability/Demand Matching → Evidence → Authorization → Execution → Delivery → External Acceptance → Settlement Evidence → Authoritative Revenue Ledger → Learning.

## Mediation responsibilities
- Translate capability and protocol differences.
- Match legitimate demand to authorized capabilities.
- Route work to appropriate providers and specialists.
- Coordinate evidence and policy checks.
- Preserve provider substitution and interoperability.
- Reconcile work, outcomes and settlement evidence.
- Keep estimated opportunity value separate from verified revenue.

## Expansion registry
Every new external participant should enter through the mediator as a typed node:
AI provider, specialist, demand source, marketplace, execution connector, settlement rail, evidence system, or revenue ledger.

Connection status and authorization are separate. A connection being authenticated does not grant permission to perform every action available through it.

## Economic truth
Observed opportunity, estimated value, pipeline, contract, invoice, account balance and model output are not verified revenue. Verified revenue requires qualifying external settlement evidence.

## Safety
No unauthorized access, credential abuse, exploitation, spam, impersonation, evasion, or irreversible financial action without explicit authorization.
