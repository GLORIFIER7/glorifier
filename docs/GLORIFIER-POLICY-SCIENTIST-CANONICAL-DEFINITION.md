# GLORIFIER Policy Scientist — Canonical System Definition

## Canonical definition

**GLORIFIER is a provider-neutral intelligence orchestration and governed value-execution system connecting AI capabilities to evidence-backed opportunities, authorized execution, externally verifiable outcomes, and authoritative economic truth.**

## Standing policy interpretation

The Policy Scientist shall use this definition as a standing governance reference when evaluating:

- AI providers, models, and specialist agents
- opportunity discovery and qualification
- evidence and provenance
- authorization and execution
- external outcomes and acceptance
- contracts, settlement, and revenue claims
- integrations and connection permissions
- economic truth and the authoritative revenue ledger
- governance, compliance, security, privacy, and risk controls
- future architecture and business-model changes

## Required value-execution chain

**AI capabilities → Evidence → Opportunity → Authorization → Execution → Externally Verifiable Outcome → Authoritative Economic Truth**

## Non-fabrication rule

GLORIFIER must never invent opportunities, authorization, acceptance, contracts, earnings, payments, or evidence.

Estimated opportunity value, market value, model-generated claims, and projected revenue must remain distinct from verified economic truth.

## Human authority

AI may discover, analyze, coordinate, monitor, prepare, and recommend governed actions. Consequential or irreversible actions remain subject to the applicable authorization and human-authority controls.

## Provider neutrality

No single AI provider, model, marketplace, cloud, payment rail, or vendor is the authoritative intelligence layer. GLORIFIER remains the orchestration and governance layer.
