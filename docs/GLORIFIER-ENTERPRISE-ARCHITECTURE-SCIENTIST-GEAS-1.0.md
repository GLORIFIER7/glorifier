# GLORIFIER Enterprise Architecture Scientist — GEAS-1.0

This layer extracts useful architecture principles from Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and IBM and adapts them to GLORIFIER rather than copying vendor-specific implementations.

## Google-derived capabilities

Google's Well-Architected Framework emphasizes operational excellence, security/privacy/compliance, reliability, cost optimization, performance and sustainability, with AI/ML as a cross-pillar perspective. GLORIFIER converts those into architecture quality gates and continuous assessment.

## Microsoft-derived capabilities

Microsoft's AI architecture separates client, intelligence API, orchestration/agent compute, knowledge, inference and tools layers. GLORIFIER adopts this separation while remaining provider-neutral. AI gateways, policy enforcement, model lifecycle, testing/evaluation, observability and responsible-agent safeguards are treated as first-class concerns.

## IBM-derived capabilities

IBM architecture and governance patterns emphasize model/data lineage, governance across deployment environments, explainability, monitoring, cataloging, privacy/IP controls and lifecycle governance. GLORIFIER incorporates these into its evidence, trust, compliance and model-governance layers.

## What is deliberately not copied

Vendor-specific services, proprietary implementation details, cloud lock-in and certification claims are not treated as GLORIFIER architecture requirements.

## Continuous operation

The Enterprise Architecture Scientist continuously assesses architecture findings, tracks evidence, identifies gaps, and feeds recommendations into the AI CEO, Specialist Council and architecture quality gates.

It may recommend changes automatically, but irreversible production changes remain governed and human-authorized.
