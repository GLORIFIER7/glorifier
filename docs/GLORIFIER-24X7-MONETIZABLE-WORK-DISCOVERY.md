# GLORIFIER 24/7 Monetizable Work Discovery Policy

## Permanent decision
GLORIFIER delegates continuous opportunity discovery to all registered AI models/agents and all monetization scientists. The system searches configured public/API sources, records evidence, creates monetization opportunity records, and queues each new finding for the full registered model/agent fleet and all monetization scientists.

## Operating loop
DISCOVER → FILTER → VERIFY → QUALIFY → EVIDENCE → VALUE → PRIORITIZE → ASSIGN → ACT → MEASURE → LEARN → REPEAT

## Delegation
- Every registered model/agent receives a discovery assignment for each new finding.
- Every monetization scientist receives a discovery assignment for each new finding.
- A primary scientist is selected for opportunity creation based on source category.
- Assignments are persisted in `glorifier_discovery_assignments` for auditable collaboration.

## Economic truth
- Discovery is evidence-producing, not revenue-producing.
- Observed opportunities and estimates are never verified earnings.
- A source URL is evidence of observation, not proof of a contract, customer, payment, or revenue.
- Revenue requires qualifying economic evidence.
- Settlement requires external provider confirmation.

## Continuous operation
- The server runs a discovery cycle at `GLORIFIER_DISCOVERY_INTERVAL_MS`.
- Default interval: 10 minutes, with a minimum of 60 seconds.
- The first autonomous discovery cycle starts shortly after server startup.
- Sources are provider-neutral and may be extended through `GLORIFIER_DISCOVERY_SOURCES`.

## Authority boundary
Agents may autonomously discover, analyze, classify, assign, and prepare opportunities. Consequential external actions remain governed and authorized; discovery does not grant authority to sign contracts, move funds, broadcast wallet transactions, or otherwise perform irreversible actions.

## Core rule
GLORIFIER never invents opportunities, contracts, earnings, or payments. It continuously searches for legitimate monetizable work and preserves the evidence trail needed for human and AI review.
