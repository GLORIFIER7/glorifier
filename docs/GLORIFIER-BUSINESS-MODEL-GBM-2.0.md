# GLORIFIER Business Model — GBM-2.0

## Executive definition

**GLORIFIER is the governed intelligence layer for AI-powered businesses.**

It is a provider-neutral intelligence operating system that connects AI models, agents, business systems, assets, data, customers and opportunities under common governance, evidence and human authority.

The business model is intentionally multi-engine rather than dependent on one monetization mechanism.

## Research synthesis

This architecture synthesizes recurring patterns visible across modern software, cloud marketplaces, AI products, platforms, data businesses, professional services and agentic systems:

- subscriptions and recurring revenue
- usage/consumption pricing
- hybrid subscription + usage
- enterprise contracts and negotiated private offers
- free trials/freemium
- per-seat and per-tenant pricing
- API/request/token/work-unit pricing
- tiered and volume pricing
- feature-based pricing
- professional and managed services
- integrations and implementation
- data/API products
- marketplaces and transaction take rates
- licensing and white-label distribution
- partner/referral economics
- outcome-linked pricing where outcomes are measurable and contractually defined
- agent/workflow-as-a-service
- embedded intelligence

AWS Marketplace documents subscription, usage, contract, private-offer, free-trial, BYOL and contract-plus-consumption patterns. Stripe documents recurring, tiered and hybrid models. McKinsey's AI-software analysis highlights the continued role of subscriptions combined with consumption pricing as AI usage expands.

## Economic truth architecture

### NOT VERIFIED
Forecasts, estimates, pipeline value, market value, model outputs, projected ROI and scenario values.

### EVIDENCE-BACKED
Supported by identifiable source evidence but not yet independently verified as a financial outcome.

### VERIFIED
Qualifying source evidence confirms the claimed event or financial result.

Permanent rules:

- Estimated value ≠ revenue.
- Market value ≠ revenue.
- Expected value ≠ realized value.
- Pipeline ≠ contracted revenue.
- Contract value ≠ collected cash.
- Missing evidence ≠ zero.
- AI output ≠ evidence.
- A valuation estimate ≠ transaction price.
- A forecast ≠ actual result.

GLORIFIER should not optimize for the largest number of monetization mechanisms.

**customer value × trust × evidence × repeatability × margin × distribution × retention**
