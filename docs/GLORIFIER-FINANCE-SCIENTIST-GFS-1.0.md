# GLORIFIER Finance Scientist — Institutional Finance Intelligence

## Purpose

Build an evidence-first finance intelligence capability inspired by the *functional categories* visible in institutional platforms and disciplined long-term capital allocation, without copying proprietary systems or treating GLORIFIER as a regulated investment manager.

Reference capability categories include BlackRock Aladdin's whole-portfolio analytics, multi-asset risk, factor analysis, scenario/stress testing, portfolio construction, performance attribution, governance and API-driven workflows. Berkshire Hathaway's public reporting also illustrates disciplined capital allocation and analysis of insurance float economics.

## Capabilities

1. Whole-portfolio intelligence
   - equities, bonds, ETFs, funds, cash, crypto, private assets, IP, operating assets and liabilities
   - consolidated exposures, concentration, liquidity and currency views

2. Risk engine
   - factor exposure
   - volatility and drawdown analytics
   - concentration and correlation
   - liquidity risk
   - counterparty/provider risk
   - scenario and stress testing
   - tail-risk and sensitivity analysis

3. Capital allocation laboratory
   - compare reinvestment, liquidity preservation, debt reduction, acquisition, R&D and other capital-use scenarios
   - opportunity-cost analysis
   - hurdle-rate and return-on-capital frameworks
   - downside-first review
   - capital allocation journal

4. Fundamental intelligence
   - revenue quality
   - margins and unit economics
   - cash conversion
   - recurring vs non-recurring revenue
   - customer concentration
   - balance-sheet strength
   - free-cash-flow analysis
   - working-capital dynamics

5. Valuation laboratory
   - DCF
   - revenue/EBITDA/earnings multiples
   - comparable-company analysis
   - scenario-weighted valuation
   - asset-based valuation
   - sum-of-the-parts
   - private-company evidence-backed valuation
   - explicit uncertainty ranges

6. Portfolio construction research
   - allocation experiments
   - diversification analysis
   - risk-budget experiments
   - rebalancing simulations
   - benchmark-relative analysis
   - no autonomous order execution

7. Insurance/float-style analysis
   - only when actual insurance data exists
   - premium, claims, expense and float evidence
   - cost-of-float analysis
   - duration/liquidity analysis
   - no invented underwriting results

8. Macro and market intelligence
   - rates, inflation, FX, commodities, credit, geopolitical and sector scenarios
   - event-to-asset exposure mapping
   - market-regime classification
   - source freshness tracking

9. Financial forensic layer
   - reconcile reported figures
   - detect anomalies
   - flag unsupported claims
   - distinguish accounting earnings, cash flow, market value and verified cash receipts

10. Decision governance
   - every material recommendation carries evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and timestamp
   - model disagreement is surfaced
   - missing evidence is not zero
   - estimates are not verified outcomes
   - human approval required for consequential financial actions

- AI CEO

It can consume GLORIFIER's asset registry, valuation engine, revenue ledger, opportunity engine, work-unit telemetry, SaaS data, IoT data and external market evidence.

## Safety boundary

This is an analytical and decision-support capability. It does not independently buy, sell, trade, withdraw, transfer, borrow, lend, contract, or move funds. Any future execution layer must remain separately permissioned and human-authorized.

## Economic truth

- Market value is not revenue.
- Estimated return is not realized return.
- Estimated valuation is not a transaction price.
- A forecast is not an actual result.
- Missing evidence is not zero.
- Verified financial outcomes require qualifying source evidence.
